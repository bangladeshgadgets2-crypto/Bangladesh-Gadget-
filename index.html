Why pricing looks different from local shops

Because every item is imported individually, each listing shows a product price plus a shipping charge calculated from its weight. Heavier items (laptops, monitors) cost more to fly or ship than light ones (earbuds, cables) — the breakdown is shown before you pay, with no hidden fees added later.

Common questions

Why is a gadget here more expensive than the same one locally? +
Because it's purchased new from a US source and shipped to you individually, the price reflects the product cost plus weight-based freight — both shown on the listing before checkout, rather than a bulk-imported local markup.
How long does delivery actually take? +
Air freight typically takes 7–14 days door to door. Economy sea freight (cargo ship) takes 25–35 days and costs less — you choose the option at checkout.
Is the first-order free shipping automatic? +
Yes — it's applied automatically at checkout on the first order placed by a new account, no code needed.
What if I receive the wrong or a fake item? +
Contact support with your order ID and photos. If a product is ever shown to be counterfeit, the ৳500,000 guarantee applies in full, in addition to a refund or replacement.
